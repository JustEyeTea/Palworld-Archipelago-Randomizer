Palworld Archipelago Randomizer

Archipelago links games from different players into one shared randomizer: the items you find can belong to someone else's game, and theirs to yours. In Palworld, the Technology tree becomes a shop whose cards send items to the other players, and the map opens region by region as passes arrive. It is for Palworld 1.0.5 on Steam for Windows, with single-player worlds, and it is new and being playtested.

No release yet: the first is coming soon.

Make your YAML Releases

Play in four steps

  1. Download the mod and palworld.apworld from Releases.
  2. Install UE4SS and the mod with the setup guide.
  3. Make your player YAML on the options page.
  4. The host generates the multiworld with the Archipelago Launcher and palworld.apworld (archipelago.gg can't generate Palworld itself), and can host it by uploading the result to archipelago.gg. Then connect from the Archipelago menu on Palworld's title screen, with your player name as the Slot.
