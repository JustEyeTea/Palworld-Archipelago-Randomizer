Palworld Archipelago glow pieces: spec
Made by Claude (Opus 5.5), an AI model by Anthropic, at JustEyeTea's direction. Rebuilt by src/glow_export.py.

Units: cm, Z up, UE axes (FBX unit = cm, so import without converting units).
Every material is UNLIT ADDITIVE, two-sided, no shadows. Textures are light on black.
"mask" = greyscale: import with sRGB off and multiply by a tint. "colour" = RGB: sRGB on, use as is.
UV: U runs left to right on the image; "the bottom of the image" is its bottom row as viewed.

THE CHECK GLOW (unvisited fast-travel statues first; alpha Pals and tower entrances later). Brand colours.
Reference object: 100 cm wide, 200 cm tall.
Scale X and Y by (object width / 100) and Z by (object height / 200).
The pillar takes the X/Y scale only and keeps its height.

SM_APGlowRing  the halo ring tube.
  Radius 95 cm, tube radius 3.5 cm, centre 110 cm above the origin.
  Tilted 14 deg about X, then -6 deg about Y (baked in).
  Origin at the object's base, centred.
  UV: U = once round the ring, V = once round the tube.
  T_APGlowRing (colour, 1024x64): the four brand colours along U; even along V.
  Motion: turn about Z at 45 deg/s (the baked tilt makes it sway).
  Glow 2.6, times |N.V|^0.6 (soft edges).
SM_APGlowSprite  the soft light: a flat quad facing +X, 240 cm square.
  Origin at its CENTRE, so turning it to the camera keeps it in place.
  Place it at the ring's centre (110 cm up at the reference size).
  UV: the whole image, U along +Y, V up.
  T_APGlowSprite (colour, 256x256): a soft cream glow. Glow 1.2.
Twinkles round the object (optional; the twinkle quad below):
  9 of them, cream, 11-21 cm across.
  70-120 cm from the centre,
  at 10%-85% of the object's height.
  They drift round at 18 deg/s.
  Each pulses between 60% and 100% size every 1.0 s.
  Glow 3.0.
SM_APGlowBeam  the pillar: an open cylinder (no caps).
  Radius 38 cm, 2000 cm tall, rings bunched near the base. Origin at the base, centred.
  UV: U = once round, V = up (the bottom of the image at the base).
  T_APGlowBeam (mask, 256x512): bright at the base, fading up, faint streaks round.
  Tint #FFD37C (brand gold). Glow 2.6, times |N.V|^1.6.
  Suggested: fade the pillar in from 10 m to 25 m away,
  so up close the halo shows alone.

THE ITEM-ARRIVAL BURST round the player: 1.25 s.
Sizes as they are (no scaling); origin at the player's feet.
Tint by the type of item received: progression #AF99EF, useful #6D8BE8, filler #00EEEE. Traps show as filler.

SM_APGlowShock  the ground shock ring: a flat disc facing up, 100 cm across.
  It sits 2 cm above the origin.
  UV: the whole image, U along +X, V along +Y.
  T_APGlowShock (mask, 512x512): a thin bright ring at 90% of the radius, with a soft trail inside it.
  Motion: from 0 to 0.75 s its width grows from 90 to 810 cm, eased out.
  (The bright front goes from 40 to 364 cm radius.)
  k = 1 - (1 - t/0.75)^3; glow 3.5 x (1 - k)^1.2. Gone after 0.75 s.
SM_APGlowColumn  the column flash: an open cylinder.
  Radius 42 cm, 260 cm tall at full size.
  Origin at the base. UV: U = once round, V = up (the bottom of the image at the base).
  T_APGlowColumn (mask, 256x512): bright at the base, fading up to nothing at the top edge.
  Rise: Z scale grows 0 -> 1 from 0 to 0.3125 s, eased out (cubic).
  Fade: from 0.25 s, fade = 1 - (t - 0.25)/0.6875.
  Glow 1.1 x fade^1.5. Gone at 0.94 s.
  Thin: over the fade, the X/Y scale shrinks by 0.4 a second (1 -> 0.72).
  Glow also times |N.V|^2.2.
SM_APGlowTwinkle  one twinkle: a flat quad facing +X, 20 cm square.
  Origin at its CENTRE (turn it to the camera). UV: the whole image, U along +Y, V up.
  T_APGlowTwinkle (mask, 128x128): a four-point sparkle with a soft glow.
  Motion: 16 twinkles.
  Each starts at a random time in 0.0-0.375 s and lives 0.75 s.
  (All are gone by 1.12 s.) Over its life, with e = 1 - (1 - life)^3:
    angle = random start + 137.5 deg x life
    reach = 35 + 50 x e x random(0.6..1.0) cm
    height = 20 + 220 x e x random(0.7..1.0) cm
    size = random(15..27) cm x (1 - life)^0.7
  Glow 4.0.
Optional: a rim flash on the player in the tint, fading out over 0.625 s.
